Our Commitment to Quality

At an altitude of 1050 meters in Suntikoppa, Coorg, Karnataka, India, our shade grown coffee epitomizes the intersection of tradition and quality.

Established in 1892 and family managed since 1949, Sandalwood Estate has earned its reputation both locally and internationally, with our premium beans delighting coffee enthusiasts worldwide.

Indulge in the rich legacy and exquisite flavors of Sandalwood Estate coffee – a true reflection of Coorg’s celebrated coffee heritage and timeless craftsmanship,

Welcome to Sandalwood Estate!

Nestled in the heart of the premier coffee growing region, Sandalwood Estate offers the perfect environment for cultivating premium, high quality coffee. Its rich soil, ideal climate, and expert farming practices come together to produce coffee that stands out for its exceptional flavor and aroma.

Sustainability Initiatives

Sustainability is at the core of everything we do at Sandalwood Estate.

We dedicate ourselves in the protection of the pristine natural beauty and rich resources of Coorg as we responsibly cultivate premium agricultural products.

Water Conservation

Through advanced water conservation systems, we prioritize the responsible and efficient use of this precious resource.

From rainwater harvesting to eco friendly irrigation methods, our sustainable practices are thoughtfully designed to preserve and protect water sources for generations to come.

Biodiversity Preservation

Our Estate thrives as a sanctuary for a diverse ecosystem, featuring majestic trees that have stood for over a century.

We are deeply committed to preserving this rich biodiversity, fostering a harmonious balance between sustainable cultivation and environmental conservation.
